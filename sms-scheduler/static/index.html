Client Texts

Upload your client list, write one message, and it goes out from your own phones a little at a time through the day.

1Your phones

Connect a phone
Install SMS Gateway for Android on the phone, switch on Cloud server, and copy the username and password it shows. Texts go out from that phone's own number and plan. iPhones can't do this — Apple doesn't let apps send texts on their own.

2Client list and message

Use {first_name}, {name} or any column from your file in curly brackets.

3Schedule

Texts only go out between 8am and 9pm. If today's window has passed, the send starts tomorrow. Keep this app running while it sends.
